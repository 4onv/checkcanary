## First-version boundary

The implementation checks whether the team's command catches the supplied defects. It stages fixtures without modifying their source, records both clean controls, and leaves unrelated failures inconclusive. It retains output counts and matched-evidence counts in reports while keeping command output local. The bundled fixtures demonstrate two deliberate checkout defects. All demonstration inputs are synthetic. Passing results describe those selected cases and their configured evidence.
